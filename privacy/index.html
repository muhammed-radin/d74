Legal / Privacy

Privacy policy.

D74 Edits is a free football clip and live TV site. You can watch without an account, downloads need no password, and we do not sell personal information. This page explains the small amount of data that is needed to run the service, and the choices you have.

Last updated 22 November 2026 Applies to d74.onrender.com No account needed to watch

The short version

  • We collect the technical data needed to serve pages, protect the service and count visits. Raw IP addresses are not kept in analytics.
  • An account is optional. If you create one, we store your display name, email address and a salted password hash — never the password itself.
  • YouTube embeds, Google AdSense (when ads are enabled) and our hosting providers process some data under their own policies. You can control ad personalisation.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time.

01 Who we are

D74 Edits (“D74”, “we”, “us”) is a free football media site that publishes clips, highlights and links to live streams. The service is operated by the individual maintainer of the D74 Edits project. For privacy questions, use the contact details at the end of this policy.

This policy covers the website at d74.onrender.com and any other address that serves the same D74 Edits application. It does not cover third-party sites, apps or players that we link to or embed.

02 What we collect

Information you give us

  • Optional account details. If you sign up, we store your display name, email address, a random salt and a salted password hash. The plain password is never stored or written to logs.
  • Messages you send. If you contact us about privacy, a takedown or a bug, we receive whatever you include in that message.
  • Administrator credentials. Site administrators have separate accounts. These are not public accounts and are protected by server-side sessions.

Information collected automatically

  • Request data. Our server and hosting provider process the IP address, browser user agent, requested path, referrer, timestamp and response status needed to deliver a page and keep the service secure.
  • Usage counts. Page views and unique visitors are counted with a salted hash of the IP address rather than the raw address. Aggregate counters help us understand which clips and pages are useful.
  • Error and security logs. Failed requests, rate-limit hits, sign-in attempts and administrator actions may be logged with masked IP addresses so we can investigate abuse.
  • Download requests. When you request a free download, the server records the request so it can rate-limit abuse and report errors. We do not require a password, PIN or account for downloads.

Information stored on your device

  • Your chosen theme and colour preference.
  • Your optional signed-in profile (display name and email) and locally saved clips or settings.
  • A temporary guest name if you have not signed in. Guest profiles stay in your browser.

We do not ask for payment details. D74 Edits is free and does not process card or bank information. If that ever changes, this policy and the checkout flow will be updated first.

03 Cookies and local storage

We use a small number of essential cookies and browser storage entries. We do not use advertising cookies ourselves; third-party embeds and ad partners may set their own.

  • Essential session cookies. Administrator sign-in uses a secure, HTTP-only session cookie. A CSRF cookie protects state-changing requests in the administrator area.
  • Preference storage. Your theme, accent colour, optional profile and saved clips are kept in localStorage on your device so the site remembers them.
  • Third-party cookies. YouTube, Google AdSense and other embedded services may set cookies or similar identifiers when you play a video or see an ad. Their policies apply to that processing.

You can clear cookies and site data from your browser settings at any time. Clearing essential cookies signs administrators out and resets local preferences, but does not delete your account.

04 How we use information

  • To deliver pages, clips, live streams and free downloads.
  • To create and secure optional accounts and administrator sessions.
  • To protect the service against spam, scraping, rate-limit abuse and unauthorised access.
  • To diagnose errors and improve performance, navigation and content.
  • To respond to requests, takedown notices and legal obligations.
  • To show advertising, where enabled, and to measure whether ad placements work.

Where data protection law uses “legal bases”, we rely on the performance of the service you request, our legitimate interest in keeping it secure and useful, your consent where it is required (for example for non-essential cookies or personalised ads), and compliance with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, rent email lists, or use your account email to send marketing without your agreement.

05 Who we share it with

We share only what is needed for a provider to perform its job. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Render hosts the application and terminates HTTPS traffic.
  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud stores site records such as clips, channels and optional accounts.
  • YouTube / Google serves embedded videos, thumbnails and live players.
  • RapidAPI supplies YouTube and football metadata used to build the public feed.
  • Redis or a managed key-value provider caches pages and stores administrator sessions when configured.
  • Google AdSense and its partners serve ads when advertising is enabled.
  • GitHub hosts the source code and handles private security or privacy reports.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights or safety of users, or as part of a transfer of the project. We do not disclose account details to advertisers.

06 Advertising and third-party embeds

D74 Edits may show advertising to keep the site free. When ads are enabled, Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies or device identifiers to select and measure ads. Depending on your region and consent choices, ads may be personalised.

  • You can manage Google ad personalisation at My Ad Center.
  • You can opt out of many participating ad networks at aboutads.info or Your Online Choices.
  • YouTube videos are embedded through youtube-nocookie.com where possible. Playing a video still contacts Google and is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not control the cookies that a third-party player or ad network sets. Use your browser controls or the links above if you want to limit them.

07 How long we keep it

  • Account records are kept until you ask us to delete the account, or until the project stops operating.
  • Request and error logs are kept for a short operational window, normally no more than 30 days, unless they are needed to investigate an incident.
  • Aggregate analytics are kept in summarised form for up to 12 months. They do not identify you individually.
  • Administrator audit entries are kept long enough to review security events, then rotated or deleted.
  • Local browser data stays on your device until you clear it.

08 How we protect it

Passwords are hashed with scrypt and a per-account salt. Administrator sessions are server-side, expire on idle and absolute timeouts, and are protected by CSRF tokens, secure cookies and rate limits. Database credentials and API keys stay on the server and are never sent to the browser.

No service can promise perfect security. If you believe you have found a vulnerability, please report it privately rather than posting it publicly.

09 Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port or restrict the processing of your personal information, to object to certain uses, and to withdraw consent. You can also ask us not to send you messages.

  • Access and correction: ask for a copy of your account details or ask us to fix them.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your account and associated records. Some security logs may be retained for a limited period where the law requires it.
  • Advertising: use the Google and industry opt-outs linked above.
  • Complaints: you may contact your local data protection authority. We would appreciate the chance to resolve the issue first.

To exercise a right, use the private contact route in the next section. We may need to confirm that the request relates to you before acting on it.

10 Children

D74 Edits is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account or sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. Parents and guardians can use browser controls to limit access to the site and third-party embeds.

11 International transfers

Our hosting, database, caching and advertising providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, those providers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. By using the service, you understand that your information may be processed outside your country of residence.

12 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or the law changes. The “last updated” date at the top of the page shows when it was revised. Material changes will be announced on the site where practical. Continuing to use D74 Edits after an update means you accept the revised policy.

13 Contact

For privacy requests, account deletion, a data question or a security report, please use a private channel so personal details are not posted in public:

Private privacy & security reports

Open a private report at github.com/muhammed-radin/d74/security/advisories/new. Include “Privacy” in the title and the email address of the account you are asking about.

General, non-sensitive questions can go to the public issue tracker: github.com/muhammed-radin/d74/issues.

We aim to respond to privacy requests within 30 days.
